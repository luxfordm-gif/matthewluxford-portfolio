The brief.

JLR was running multiple disconnected design teams across web, native, in-vehicle and dealer tooling. Engineering shipped slowly. Brand was inconsistent. The user journey from "I want a new car" to "I'm sitting in one" had a dozen seams.

I came in to lead UX, UI and the establishment of a shared design system — Tokens, components, patterns, accessibility baked in — that the whole organisation could ship from.

What we shipped.

A foundational token system and component library covering web, iOS and Android. A redesigned vehicle configurator that became the highest-converting page on the site. New native experiences for owners. A retailer tool that replaced three legacy systems.

I led a team of twelve, mentored juniors, and worked alongside engineering leadership to make the system genuinely self-serve.
