About

Eighteen years building thoughtful digital products that scale.

I'm Matthew Luxford, a senior web designer, design lead and go-to designer based in Surrey, UK. I work end-to-end across UX, UI, brand and design systems, sitting comfortably between users, business and engineering. Currently freelance and open to senior roles in 2026 — remote or hybrid, worldwide.

I'm a dad of two and a recently converted road cyclist (apologies in advance). When I was permanent, I was always chasing the next job title, grinding towards design director. Since going contract, that's changed — any role is a role, and I genuinely love getting stuck in. Full-cycle work from the first kickoff brief through to delivery is where I'm happiest. I've worn a lot of different hats across my career, and that's given me real experience communicating with developers, helping teams build in various CMSs, and translating between design and code without the ego.

More recently, I've been actively exploring AI — not as a replacement, but as a tool. I'll use it wherever it makes me more efficient and helps deliver better work, whether that's design, code generation, or anything in between.
