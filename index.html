About

Eighteen years building thoughtful digital products that scale.

I'm Matthew Luxford, a senior web designer, design lead and go-to designer based in Surrey, UK. I work end-to-end across UX, UI, brand and design systems. Currently freelance and open to senior roles in 2026 — remote or hybrid, worldwide.

Dad of two, recently converted road cyclist. When permanent, I chased design director titles. Since going contract (10 years ago), any role is a role — full-cycle work from brief to delivery is where I'm happiest. I've got real experience communicating with clients, developers and translating between design and code.

More recently, exploring AI as a tool to work more efficiently and deliver better work.

2024 – Present
Kraken, Senior Product Designer. Remote · Crypto
Designing for the global crypto exchange — trading, onboarding and the systems that make complex finance feel obvious.
2021 – 2022
Yoppie, Lead Product Designer (Contract). London
Head of design and creative oversight across all digital comms for the women's-health DTC.
2020 – 2021
Spark44 (Jaguar Land Rover), Lead Product Designer (Contract). London · NDA

Led ground-up component rebuild with efficiency at its core. We identified poor-performing components caused by responsive breakpoint issues and text overlays constraining vehicle imagery. As one of two senior leads on a six-person team, rebuilt the site piece-by-piece over 6 months, creating reusable components to ensure the platform was future-proof and scalable. Contracted in to run Figma workshops, set up the component library, and established Principle animation patterns for interactive states and transitions.

Overhauled 'Models & Specifications' pages for JLR's global site across 27 markets. Led the design, ran competitor analysis, and made data-driven UX/UI calls. The trick was surfacing what users needed to pick the right vehicle while staying flexible across both brands' complex model ranges. Every decision served the user first.

2020 – 2021

Strategic redesign of the booking flow — analytics showed a 500% increase in booking enquiries in the first month post-launch. Brand refresh and native iOS/Android app overhaul.
